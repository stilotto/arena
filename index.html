The Hundred

Ten of every calling. One arena between the planes.

Vhorax, Editor of the Games, was a warlord of the Iron Plane until he lost his army and his throne with it. He cannot go home without a legion, so he has built a forge for one.

One hundred heroes were taken from the mortal world in a single night. Each day the gates open on something worse. Whoever is still standing at the end will be worth leading. The dead stay dead.
