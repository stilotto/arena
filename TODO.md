# The Hundred: to do

Ideas from the owner. Nothing here is built until the owner says go.

## Built (kept here as a record)

- Siege from one to four gates, with great doors the foes march through in the
  skycam and the legion ringed at the centre, tough callings outermost.
- Cohort assembly: move heroes between cohorts by tap or drag. Gear now belongs
  to a calling, so it follows heroes into any cohort.
- Zone of control: entering a square next to an enemy ends the move, and
  walking away from an enemy gives it a free blow. Thieves and assassins slip
  away; fliers ignore it.
- Deployment by hand before the first turn.
- Vhorax as the day 31 foe, with two endings (kneel, or defy and win).
- Late days field tougher veterans instead of ever-larger hordes.

- The board takes the full height on a wide screen, with the panel, controls
  and log in a sidebar on the right. Phones keep the stacked layout.

- Sound made in code (no audio files): blows, arrows, each spell, deaths,

  level-up. One button cycles off, low and full, and is remembered.
- Pointing at a square shows hit chance and damage, or what a move costs.
- A hand-made move can be undone until the hero acts, if nothing came of it.
- A Guard order: hold position but step up to meet a foe who comes close.
  Cohorts on Attack switch to Guard on siege days and back afterwards.
- Vhorax comments at dawn and after each day.
- Service records: fights, times cut down, times raised, champions slain.
- A battle in progress is saved, and Continue returns to it.

## Sprint 2a: mechanics (built)

- **Unaware foes and patrols.** Everyone in a quest room starts unaware. A
  foe notices a hero in plain view within six squares (two for a thief or
  assassin, three for a ranger) and loses the round it notices in; those it
  shouts awake lose that round too. Blows on the unwary are at +4, and a
  thief's is a backstab. Fire, lightning and a forced lid make noise. One or
  two foes in a room walk a beat.
- **Traps and locked chests.** Thieves and assassins spot traps from two
  squares, step over them, and disarm them from the next square. Anyone else
  who walks over one sets it off: spikes, a snare, or an alarm that wakes the
  room. A known trap is never walked through by accident; it takes a second
  tap. Chests: a thief picks the lock quietly, others force it and the noise
  carries; some have a needle in the lock. Chest gold joins the quest takings.
- **Potions and wands.** Giant Strength, Speed, Stoneskin and Shadows, from
  chests, doors and the quartermaster. Wands of Missiles, Webs, Binding and
  Fire hold charges; only callings with a spellbook can use them. The
  quartermaster sells one wand a day.
- **Monster abilities.** Dire wolves, gutter hounds and minotaurs charge: a
  run of three squares doubles the first blow, and a hero on Guard who meets
  the charge strikes double instead. Zombies, oozes, bruisers and the clay
  guardian grab and hold. Wolves, hounds, cutpurses, cultists and apprentices
  call for help: they rouse the whole room, and when badly hurt may bring one
  more (two a fight at most).

Open after 2a:

- None of it has been played by hand. The sims still pass, but sneaking,
  trap damage and chest takings are first guesses.
- Charge, grab and the wolves' call also apply in The Hundred's arena.
- Quick fight never drinks the new potions, uses wands, or opens chests.
- Chests and traps are not shown in the skycam.
- Foes ignore traps altogether.

## Sprint 2b: places (built)

Three more quests, six in all. Each has six kinds of room and a master.

- **The Under-Drains**: brick tunnels cut up by channels of green water, with
  plank crossings, settling tanks and a weir. Rats, giant leeches (grab),
  cutpurses, oozes, the drowned, wererats, sewer crocodiles (charge); the
  Rat Piper, who calls rats four at a time.
- **The Lantern Market**: a square between house fronts under strings of
  paper lanterns: stalls, a fountain, an arcade, beast pens, a cloth hall.
  No rain. Thugs, cutpurses, sellswords, mountebanks (who throw from five
  squares), hounds, bruisers, duellists; Madam Sable the fence and her hired
  swords. House doors can be tried here too.
- **The Black Gull**: a corsair galley at the wharf. The room is the hull,
  with the river round it: main deck with masts and yards, gun deck, hold,
  galley, berths, and the foredeck. Deckhands (call for help), corsairs,
  harpooners, bosuns (grab), the drowned; Captain Marrow.

Open after 2b:

- Each new place borrows an older one's way of drawing (drains: crypt;
  market: street; ship: tower), with its own colours and props. They could
  each take more of their own art.
- The new places have no sound of their own: drains use the crypt's drips,
  the market the crowd murmur, the ship the tower's wind.
- Foes and masters are priced by guess. The party sim clears them at about
  the same rate as the old three.
- No bookshelves or sarcophagi in the new places; chests, traps and (in the
  market) doors only.

## The Six: built

The party mode is in: party creation, hand control in initiative order, a
spellbook with daily memorising, individual gear with a paper doll, loot, a
daily quartermaster, raising the dead, figures on the board, and a quick-fight
button. Choices made where the notes were open:

- A hero cut down is only "down" unless the blow takes them to ten below
  nothing; the downed get up after the fight. The truly dead need raising.
- The temple raises for 60 gp plus 60 per level. A cleric of 9th level raises
  one hero a day for free. Being raised costs the hero nothing.
- Losing a day forfeits half the gold; the downed are spared.
- A party meets at most two gates, never three or four.
- Casters memorise 1 + level spells a day. New spells: Web, Bless, Hold Person.

Still open for The Six:

- Balance is from the headless sim only. On quick fight it clears days 1 to 19
  and then stalls on the wyrm (day 25) and Vhorax (day 31).
- Only the first ring slot fills automatically; no drag and drop on the doll.
- No initiative modifiers from gear, and no ranged-attack penalty in melee.

## Side quests for The Six: built

Vhorax grants leave for one quest before each arena day. Three places at
first (three more came in sprint 2b), each
four rooms with no rest between; the last room holds its master.

- **The Sunken Crypt**: flagstones, pillars, sarcophagi, a flooded cistern,
  torchlight. Cultists, the walking dead, oozes, gargoyles; the Bone-Speaker.
- **The Leaning Tower**: round wooden floors against a night sky, shelves,
  cauldron, cages, an open observatory. Apprentices, imps, animated armour,
  a clay guardian; Master Ilvane.
- **Gutter Row**: a narrow cobbled street between rooftops, alleys, crates and
  washing lines, ending in a yard. Thugs, cutpurses, archers, hounds, bruisers,
  wererats; Mother Crake.
- Walls block movement and line of sight.
- Rooms are generated from a seed: seven kinds of crypt room, six of tower
  floor and six of street, in varying sizes, plus a final room for each place.
- The board shows raised walls and props that figures can stand behind, light
  and shadow from torches and from the party itself, flames, dust, fog and
  rain. The skycam scene for each place has baked torchlight, pitched roofs,
  its own sky, and draws far to near by cell so nothing shows through walls.
- Between rooms: tend wounds with leftover healing, drink a potion, press on,
  or turn back with the takings. Falling or fleeing loses the takings.
- A cleared place comes back harder next time.

Still open:

- Quest experience is generous: a party that quests every day reaches about
  level 13 by day 30, against about level 9 without. Needs hands-on tuning.
