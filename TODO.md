# The Hundred: to do

Ideas from the owner. Nothing here is built until the owner says go.

## Siege from several sides

- The legion is besieged from one to four directions instead of one.
- Opponents enter through huge doors in the arena wall, and we see them come in.
- The legion starts in the centre and "circles the wagons": tough callings on
  the outside, casters and archers inside.

## Cohort assembly

- Cohorts are fixed today: one per calling, ten heroes each.
- Add an assembly screen where heroes can be dragged between cohorts, so a
  cohort can mix callings (for example fighters in front, clerics behind).

## Zone of control

- Today a unit only blocks the square it stands on. Foes cannot walk through
  heroes, but they can walk around them, and nothing stops a foe leaving a
  fight.
- Wanted: the front rank protects the back ranks. A foe next to a hero should
  be slowed or stopped when it tries to slip past.

## Smaller notes

- Vhorax does not appear as a foe yet, and nothing happens after day 30.
- Deployment is automatic; no way to place cohorts by hand.
- Days 1 to 5 are too easy and the late game is too easy once Fireball arrives.
- Late billings pad the budget with cheap foes, so some days field hundreds
  (day 24 has 160 dire wolves, day 25 has 344 goblin archers). Swap the filler
  for tougher foes so numbers stay sensible.
